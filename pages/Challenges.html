THE ENTITY'S DESCENT

A Story of Survival and Sacrifice

"The Entity doesn't want you to be perfect. It just wants to see how much pain you can take."

Time to finish: 15–25 Hours (Over 30 Hours if you dare to try the hardest modes)

Welcome to the fog! This is a massive, long-form challenge where every single choice matters. Your ultimate goal? Win a game with every single Killer. But there is a twist: if you lose a match, the game isn't over. Instead, things just get darker and harder.

Step 1: Choose Your Weapons

Before your Killer steps into the trial, you build their loadout. Think of the perks in Dead by Daylight as 8 different "schools of magic" (Disciplines). You are only allowed to pick four different schools. You cannot pick the same one twice! Once you have your four schools, choose exactly one perk from each.

Step 2: The Downward Spiral

You start your first game feeling powerful with all 4 of your chosen perks. If you win, congratulations! That Killer is completely finished. But if you lose? You begin The Descent. You must throw away one of your perks forever. If you lose again, you lose another perk. And another.

The Last Hope: The Perkless Lifeline

What happens when you lose all your perks? The Entity gives you one final chance to prove yourself. You must play a game with no perks at all.

If you win, you climb back up and earn back 1 perk!

If you lose? The Entity gets angry. Those 4 perks are banned forever for that Killer. You have to start over by drafting 4 brand new perks.

The True Nightmare: Ascension Tiers

Did you manage to win a game with every single Killer? Amazing! Now you unlock the endgame.

THE ENTITY'S ROULETTE

A 30-Hour Test of Greed, Survival, and Chance

GOAL

Survive or Perish

The goal is simple: your team must reach 20,000 Safe Points to win the whole challenge.

The rules adapt depending on how many friends are playing with you. There are no ties.

Party SizeWin ConditionLoss Condition
Squad (4)3 or 4 Survivors escape0, 1, or 2 Survivors escape
Trio (3)2 or 3 Survivors escape0 or 1 Survivor escapes
Duo (2)BOTH Survivors must escape0 or 1 Survivor escapes
Solo (1)YOU must escape (Hatch or Gates)You die

Example

If playing as a Duo, it is hardcore mode. Both must survive to win.

01

Perk Selection

Before you play, you must open the Tracker and spin for random perks. The system will give exactly 4 perks for each person playing.

Don't own all the characters in the game? No problem. Use the Tracker's Roster to turn off the characters you do not have.

02

The Ban System: Ransom Jail

If you lose a game, the perks your team used are locked away in Ransom Jail. You cannot use them again.

You can try to rescue them! Tell your team you are doing a Jailbreak Match. Win, and all trapped perks are free. Lose, and you lose 1,000 points, plus your new perks get locked too.

03

The Checkpoint System: Cash Out or Spin

When you win, you earn points in a temporary 'Pot'. You have exactly 60 seconds to make a choice:

Win 1: 200 pts | Win 2: 500 pts | Win 3: 1,000 pts | Win 4: 2,000 pts | Win 5: 4,000 pts

Choice A: Cash Out (Safe). Save the points to your Safe Bank forever. Streak goes to zero.

Choice B: Spin Again (Greed). Leave the points in the Pot and play again. Win, points grow huge. Lose, you lose all Pot points and perks go to jail.

04

The Golden Spin

Every time you spin for new perks, there is a small chance the screen will turn Gold. This is a Golden Spin!

When this happens, the points for your next match are multiplied by three. But there is a massive catch: You are not allowed to "Cash Out" after a Golden Spin game. You must play at least one more game after it and risk all those points.
