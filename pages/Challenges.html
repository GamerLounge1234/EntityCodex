THE ENTITY'S DESCENT

A Story of Survival and Sacrifice

"The Entity doesn't want you to be perfect. It just wants to see how much pain you can take."

Time to finish: 15–25 Hours (Over 30 Hours if you dare to try the hardest modes)

Welcome to the fog! This is a massive, long-form challenge where every single choice matters. Your ultimate goal? Win a game with every single Killer. But there is a twist: if you lose a match, the game isn't over. Instead, things just get darker and harder. Here is how your story unfolds.

Step 1: Choose Your Weapons

Before your Killer steps into the trial, you have to build their loadout. Think of the perks in Dead by Daylight as 8 different "schools of magic" (we call them Disciplines). You are only allowed to pick four different schools. You cannot pick the same one twice! Once you have your four schools, choose exactly one perk from each.

Step 2: The Downward Spiral

You start your first game feeling powerful with all 4 of your chosen perks. If you win, congratulations! That Killer is completely finished. But if you lose? You begin The Descent. The Entity demands a sacrifice. You must throw away one of your perks forever. You get to choose which one to lose, but you are now weaker. If you lose again, you lose another perk. And another.

The Last Hope: The Perkless Lifeline

What happens when you lose all your perks? The Entity gives you one final chance to prove yourself. You must play a game with no perks at all.

If you somehow survive and win this test, you get to climb back up! You earn back 1 perk and get to keep trying.

But if you lose this final test? The Entity gets angry. Those 4 perks you originally picked are now banned forever for that specific Killer. You have to start their story all over again by drafting 4 brand new perks.

The True Nightmare: Ascension Tiers

Did you manage to win a game with every single Killer? Amazing! But the story isn't over. Now, you unlock the endgame. These are terrifying new rules that change everything.

I

Weakened Tools

You are no longer allowed to use strong items. Only the weakest Brown and Yellow add-ons are allowed.

II

The Entity's Choice

Before you even pick your perks, the Entity randomly bans two whole Disciplines. You have to make do with what's left.

III

A Fast Fall

The punishment for losing is much faster. If you lose once, you drop straight down to 2 perks. Lose again, and you are immediately at zero.

IV

Perfect or Nothing

A normal win isn't good enough anymore. If even one survivor escapes (through the gates or the hatch), it counts as a loss. You must kill all four.

THE ENTITY'S ROULETTE

A 30-Hour Test of Greed, Survival, and Chance

"Spin the wheel. Risk your life. Escape or die trying."

GOAL

Survive or Perish

The goal is simple: your team must reach 20,000 Safe Points to win the whole challenge.

The rules adapt depending on how many friends are playing with you. There are no ties in this game. If a tie happens, the Killer wins, and you lose.

Party Size Win Condition Loss Condition
Squad (4 Players) 3 or 4 Survivors escape 0, 1, or 2 Survivors escape
Trio (3 Players) 2 or 3 Survivors escape 0 or 1 Survivor escapes
Duo (2 Players) BOTH Survivors must escape 0 or 1 Survivor escapes
Solo (1 Player) YOU must escape (Hatch or Gates) You die (Random teammates do not matter)

Example

If you are playing as a Duo (2 players), it is hardcore mode. Both of you must survive the match to win. If one of you escapes, but the other dies trying to save you, the entire match counts as a Loss.

01

Perk Selection

Before you play, you must click the button below to spin for random perks. The system will give exactly 4 perks for each person playing.

Don't own all the characters in the game? No problem. Click the "Manage Perk Inventory" button to turn off the characters you do not have. The game will never give you a perk you cannot equip.

Example

If you are playing as a Trio (3 players), the wheel will give you 12 random perks. You and your friends look at all 12 perks and decide who gets which ones. Player 1 could take all the healing perks, while Player 2 takes the running perks. You build the team strategy together.

02

The Ban System: Ransom Jail

If you lose a game, you do not just lose points. The perks your team used are locked away in Ransom Jail. You cannot use them again. As more perks go to jail, you have fewer options left.

But you can try to rescue them! Before a game starts, tell your team you are doing a Jailbreak Match. You spin for new perks and play. If you win, all trapped perks are free. If you lose, you lose 1,000 points from your bank, and your new perks get locked in jail too.

Changed Party Sizes? The jail remembers your locked perks. Even if your friends log off, you can do a Solo Jailbreak match to free the perks for later!

Example

You lose a game while using Sprint Burst and Adrenaline. Those perks are now locked in jail. Two games later, you decide to do a "Jailbreak Match" and you win. Sprint Burst and Adrenaline are now free and can show up on the wheel again.

03

The Checkpoint System: Cash Out or Spin

When you win, you earn points in a temporary 'Pot'. You have exactly 60 seconds to make a choice:

Win 1: 200 pts | Win 2: 500 pts | Win 3: 1,000 pts | Win 4: 2,000 pts | Win 5: 4,000 pts

Choice A: Cash Out (Safe). Save the points to your Safe Bank. They are yours forever, but your winning streak goes back to zero.

Choice B: Spin Again (Greed). Risk it! Leave the points in the Pot and play another match. Win, and the points grow huge. Lose, and you lose all Pot points, plus your perks go to jail.

Example

You win your first match and get 200 points. You choose "Spin Again". You win the second match, so the Pot grows to 500 points. Now you choose "Cash Out". Those 500 points are added to your Safe Bank forever. Next time you win, you will start back at 200 points.

04

The Golden Spin

Every time you spin for new perks, there is a small chance the screen will turn Gold. This is a Golden Spin!

When this happens, the points for your next match are multiplied by three. But there is a massive catch: You are not allowed to "Cash Out" after a Golden Spin game. You must play at least one more game after it and risk all those points.

Example

You are on a 2-game win streak (500 points in the Pot). You spin the wheel and get a Golden Spin! You win the match, earning you 3,000 points (1,000 points for winning game 3, multiplied by 3). But you cannot save them yet! You MUST hit "Spin Again" and risk all 3,000 points in the next game.
