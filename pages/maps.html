Wreckers' Yard (Autohaven)

Scroll to Zoom • Drag to Pan

The Foundation

Basic Information

Realm: Autohaven Wreckers

Map Name: Wreckers' Yard (Internal: "Scrapyard")

Size: 9,216 m² (144 tiles). One of the smallest Autohaven maps.

Main Landmark: Killer Shack

Unlike many maps, Wreckers' Yard has no true main building. The only guaranteed landmark is the Killer Shack.

  • Always spawns near the center of the map.
  • The Basement is guaranteed to spawn inside the Shack.
  • Surrounded by safer "filler tiles" instead of giant scrap walls.
  • Often has an elevated dirt hill nearby.

Architecture of Scrap

The map normally contains about 5 major maze tiles. Common tiles include:

  • LT Walls: The classic L-wall + T-wall combination.
  • Jungle Gyms: Long Wall, Standard, and Window variations.
  • Car Wall Tiles: Corridors made of crushed vehicles.
  • The Bus Tile: Debris surrounding a crashed bus; one of the strongest structures.
  • The Crane: A massive crane structure offering tight loops.
  • Fuel Tanker: A long vehicle tile with loops around it.
  • Dead Zones: Open junkyard sections with very limited resources once pallets are broken.

Objective Spawns

Generators

Generators use a spawn-slot system and are not fixed. Typical distribution:

  • 1 guaranteed near the Killer Shack area.
  • 2–3 near major tiles (Bus, Crane, or Tanker).
  • 2 along the map edges (scrap corners).
  • 1 in an open, risky center area.

Totems (Bone Locations)

Roughly 10–15 possible spawn slots exist, but only 5 totems will spawn per match. Check these common anchor points:

  • Behind/beside the Hill rocks.
  • Outside the perimeter walls of the Shack.
  • Hidden in the debris of the Bus, Crane, or Tanker tiles.
  • Tucked into the sharp corners of map-edge scrap walls.

Pro Tip: Veteran players look for the surrounding props (like specific trees or scrap piles) rather than the bones themselves.

Entity Placements

Survivor Spawns

Survivors usually spawn in groups of 2-4 along the map edges (North scrap wall, South junk pile, East/West edges). They rarely spawn directly beside the Shack.

Killer Spawns

The Killer usually spawns on the opposite side of the map from survivors, often near the edges, the Crane, or the Bus.

The Hatch

When the hatch spawns, common priority zones include:

  • Near the Killer Shack.
  • Adjacent to the Bus, Crane, or Tanker.
  • Tucked against outer boundary map edges.

Power Dynamics

Strong Survivor Areas

S
Shack & nearby filler tiles, The Bus Tile, Strong LT Wall combos.
A
Crane loops, Tanker loops, Long Wall Jungle Gyms.
Weak
Open scrapyard sections, empty corners, and dead zones after pallets are dropped.

Killer Advantages

Very Strong: Nurse, Blight, Spirit, Hillbilly.
Reason: The map's small/medium size combined with long lines of sight.

Strong: Huntress, Deathslinger, Artist.

Weaker: Trapper, Pig (due to high visibility of traps and small map size).

Competitive Strategy

The Opening Play

As soon as you spawn, visually locate the Shack, Bus, Crane, and Tanker. Once you identify these four structures, you can mentally divide the map into manageable quadrants.

Generator Management

To prevent a deadly 3-gen scenario, aim to repair generators spread across the strongest structures early. Break the triangle between the Shack, Bus, and Crane generators.

Efficient Totem Route

If looking for a Hex, run this perimeter sweep:
Shack → Hill → Bus → Crane → Map Edge → Tanker → Center.
