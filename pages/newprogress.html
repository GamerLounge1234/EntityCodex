The Point System (Analytics)

Your devotion is measured in Blood Points. The deeper the descent, the higher the risk, the greater the reward.

  • Flawless Victory: Win with all 4 starting perks. +1000 pts
  • The Descent: Win after sacrificing 1 perk (3 remaining). +750 pts
  • Deep Descent: Win after sacrificing 2 perks (2 remaining). +500 pts
  • Near Abyss: Win with only 1 perk remaining. +250 pts
  • The Perkless Lifeline: Survive with 0 perks. +1500 pts
  • Failure Penalty: Every loss deducts points. -100 pts

Ascension Tiers (Endgame)

Complete all 41 killers to unlock Tiers. Multipliers apply to all future points.

  • Tier I: Weakened Tools (1.5x)
  • Tier II: Entity's Choice (2.0x)
  • Tier III: Fast Fall (2.5x)
  • Tier IV: Perfect or Nothing (3.0x)
